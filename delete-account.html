Delete your PlotCue account

You can permanently delete your PlotCue account directly from the PlotCue app.

  1. Open PlotCue and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account.

Data that is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the PlotCue account and associated account data, including your profile, saved shows and Watch Later data, watched-episode progress, and account preferences.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or need assistance with account deletion, contact contact@plotcue.app.

Data retention

PlotCue does not keep your account active after you complete account deletion. Information may only be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory obligations.
