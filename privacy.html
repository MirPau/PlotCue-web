Overview

PlotCue is designed to collect only the information needed to provide and improve the app. This policy explains the information used by PlotCue and how it is handled.

Information you provide

When you create and use an account, PlotCue may store your email address, name, account settings, selected streaming region, notification preferences, shows and movies you add, and your viewing progress.

Authentication and storage

Account authentication and application data are handled through Supabase. PlotCue uses this information to keep your account and viewing progress synchronized.

TV and streaming data

PlotCue uses third-party TV metadata and streaming-availability data to display information about shows, episodes, posters, release dates, and streaming providers. Availability information can vary by region and may not always be complete or current.

Notifications

If you enable episode notifications on a supported device, PlotCue uses your notification preferences and episode release information to provide the alerts you requested.

Advertising and selling data

PlotCue is designed to be ad-free. PlotCue does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Data retention and account deletion

Account information and viewing data are retained while needed to provide the service. Signed-in users can permanently delete their PlotCue account and associated account data from within the app. For assistance, contact contact@plotcue.app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PlotCue develops or its services change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or feedback, email contact@plotcue.app.

Optional contributions

Optional native support purchases use Apple or Google Play and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your PlotCue account identifier, store purchase details and technical information used for purchase verification and purchase analytics. PlotCue stores verified transaction and product identifiers, purchase dates, store and refund status, and contribution history linked to your account. PlotCue does not receive your payment card details. Purchasing is optional and all app features remain free; opening the native support section may also initialize purchase verification. Contributions cannot be purchased on the web.

Purchase data and deletion

Delete Account removes your PlotCue account and associated app data. It does not itself refund purchases or automatically delete records held separately by Apple, Google or RevenueCat. To request erasure of RevenueCat purchase data used by PlotCue, contact contact@plotcue.app. We verify the request and handle it with the provider, subject to applicable retention requirements. Store purchases and refunds remain managed by the relevant store.

Your data rights

PlotCue stores your account details, preferences, saved shows and movies, and watched progress to provide and synchronize the service through Supabase. Native contribution verification also uses RevenueCat and your app account identifier. Where GDPR applies, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing, subject to applicable conditions. You can withdraw consent for optional processing without affecting earlier lawful processing, and complain to your supervisory authority. Contact contact@plotcue.app for data requests, including purchase data held by RevenueCat. Delete Account removes your PlotCue account and associated app data; it does not itself refund store purchases or automatically erase records held separately by Apple, Google or RevenueCat.
