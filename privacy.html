Overview

PlotCue is designed to collect only the information needed to provide and improve the app. This policy explains the information used by PlotCue and how it is handled.

Information you provide

When you create and use an account, PlotCue may store your email address, name, account settings, selected streaming region, notification preferences, shows you add, and your watched-episode progress.

Authentication and storage

Account authentication and application data are handled through Supabase. PlotCue uses this information to keep your account and viewing progress synchronized.

TV and streaming data

PlotCue uses third-party TV metadata and streaming-availability data to display information about shows, episodes, posters, release dates, and streaming providers. Availability information can vary by region and may not always be complete or current.

Notifications

If you enable episode notifications on a supported device, PlotCue uses your notification preferences and episode release information to provide the alerts you requested.

Advertising and selling data

PlotCue is designed to be ad-free. PlotCue does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Data retention and account deletion

Account information and viewing data are retained while needed to provide the service. Signed-in users can permanently delete their PlotCue account and associated account data from within the app. For assistance, contact contact@plotcue.app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PlotCue develops or its services change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or feedback, email contact@plotcue.app.
