Your shows, kept in order.
PlotCue is a TV series tracker built to make following your shows simple. Keep track of what you have watched, see what comes next, and stay on top of upcoming episodes without losing your place.
What PlotCue does
Track your progress
Mark episodes and seasons as watched and keep your progress synchronized.
Know what comes next
Continue watching from the next episode and see upcoming releases in one place.
Streaming availability
See available streaming providers based on your selected region when provider data is available.
Stay informed
On iOS and Android, choose when PlotCue should notify you about upcoming and newly released episodes.
Take PlotCue with you
Download the app to keep your shows close, wherever you go.
